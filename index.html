About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), majoring in Software Engineering. I am passionate about Large Language Models (pre-training, post-training, code agents) and AI for Software Engineering.

Currently, I am an AI research intern at Xiaohongshu Dots, working on pretrain data (code) and midtrain. Previously, I interned at Xiaomi MiMo, where I contributed to MiMo-V2.6, as well as ByteDance (Douyin), Metabit Trading, and BondiTech. I am an active contributor to open-source projects and a strong believer in the power of open-source culture.
