batch size调度策略​

2.3.1 Batch size ramp-up The optimal batch size increases throughout training. Early in training, a larger batch size wastes compute (and tokens) because a gradient estimate from a fraction of the batch’s tokens suffices, whereas later in training, we benefit from more precise gradients. Therefore, we ramp up the batch size in stages with a share of tokens that grows exponentially per stage: the final batch size (stage) runs for the last 50% of tokens, the penultimate batch size runs for 25% of tokens, and so on. We rescale the learning rate for each stage by a factor that is tuned empirically. Batch-size ramp-up also acts as a regularizer: noisier gradient estimates from the smaller early batches reduce overfitting, and the benefits of regularization increase with the number of data repeats. Like the batch-size compute multiplier, the choice of schedule trades off optimization efficiency against throughput. From an optimization perspective, we find that ramp-up from 0.25× to 2× the predicted batch size performs best empirically. However, larger batch sizes achieve higher MFU in our setting. Therefore, we chose to ramp up from 1× to 4× based on wall-clock performance, which produces the 40M, 80M, 160M schedule above

motivation是

  1. 数据不够，pretrain需要适当的重复（尤其是mistral使用了两阶段配比，第一阶段65%数据配17%code, 第二阶段35%数据配39%的code, code应该是要重复好几遍的

  2. 重复会overfit

  3. 小batch 噪声大 ，防overfit， 同时对于数据的利用较大batch更高， 所以前期用小batch更好

  4. 但是小batch会影响mfu，速度降低，所以 设scaling ladder算出来的最优batch size为x, 并没有采用从 0.25x -> 2x 的方案，而是采用了 x -> 4x的方案，优先保证速度（4000 GB200 45day 完成1t pretrain）

Sparse Attn 带来agentic性能下降？​

3.1 Agentic penalty on architecture Initially, we used multiple optimizations to reduce the KV-cache footprint of ML4, notably: • Ragged multi-head latent attention (MLA): One in every four layers uses full attention, the remaining layers use sliding-window attention (SWA) [18–20] with a window size of 512 tokens. • Cross-layer attention sharing (CLA) [21]: Every other full-attention layer reuses the KV cache of the preceding full-attention layer. • YOCO [22]: Full-attention layers in the second half of the transformer attend to the KV cache produced by the first half instead of maintaining their own KV cache. Beyond KV-cache reduction, YOCO enables two-stage prefilling, which is 1.7× faster at 1M context on our benchmarks. The combination of these optimizations reduced the KV-cache size by a factor of 16 compared to a full-attention baseline. Early ablations suggested that impact on pretraining performance, including perplexity and other common pretraining and post-training benchmarks, was either negligible or small enough to justify the efficiency gains. However, as ML4 pretraining continued, early SFT cooldowns on intermediate checkpoints revealed poor performance. Despite expected performance on pretraining evaluations, including perplexity and knowledge-based benchmarks, the model’s post-SFT performance was lower than expected on agentic evaluations, especially those that require long-context reasoning like SWE-bench [23] and BrowseComp [24]. Parallel research efforts helped us to isolate the performance regression. Under the same training token budget, SWE-bench Verified [25] scores measured after midtraining and SFT decreased as we moved from full attention to SWA, and regressed further when applying CLA

在预训指标上，yoco，swa和fa（mla）混合并没有怎么掉点的同时，带来了16x 的kvcache减少，但当训练进行到midtrain/sft阶段时，full attn却表现出来了明显的优势

好像ERNIE 5.5 flash也用了一些独特的策略, 比如 https://zhouyuxuanyx.github.io/2026/11/05/one-knob-is-not-enough-softmax-temperature-vs-multimax.html ，给对数学过敏的人（比如我）省流

softmax是单峰的，但真实注意力 or 标签预测之类可能是多峰的 本质上从 logits -> 概率需要满足两个事情

  1. 稀疏性：垃圾choice被压低
  2. （sometimes）多峰性: 合理的选择都可以高峰

而传统softmax只有一个旋钮温度T, T高多峰但不稀疏，T低稀疏但不多峰

本质上理想的做法是一个阶梯函数，

  • if 低logits -> 压低（稀疏）
  • if 中logits -> 保持
  • if 高logits -> 抬高（多峰）

，然后可以relu把if-else变得可导，再二阶化变得光滑，就得到了一些可学习的旋钮（而不只是一个T），再把这族旋钮的初始化变成softmax, 就能获取到剩下的增益

听起来相当合理，但有一个问题是，multimax在保留了多峰的同时也削弱了主峰的学习强度，对于小模型有限样本的情况下验出来效果不佳（例如在质量模型使用multimax替换softmax）
